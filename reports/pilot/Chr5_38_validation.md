# Pilot follow-up: Chr5_38

The 500 bp screen flagged Chr5_38 as its only class-B locus:
mean CDS divergence was 0.37%, and mean upstream divergence was 42.35%.

The annotated 5′ gene boundary was 45–57 bp from the representative CDS
start in Pe5 and Pe14, compared with 1,120–1,221 bp in the other four
pilot isolates. We aligned larger intervals spanning the focal CDS
anchor to the upstream neighbor.

Across the eight between-group comparisons, the original windows
shared zero columns in the larger alignment. The larger intervals
were 99.60–99.90% identical at callable bases. The original score
therefore compared different parts of a largely conserved locus.

The original class-B call is retained as a screening result.
The follow-up did not validate it as divergence between comparable
upstream windows. This does not establish that upstream variation
is absent from the unresolved loci.
