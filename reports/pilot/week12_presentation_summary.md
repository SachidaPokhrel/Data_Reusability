# Week 12 presentation

**Question.** Does coding conservation extend into upstream DNA at single-copy core loci?

**Data re-use.** The 19-isolate published pangenome defined the core set. The pilot sampled 100 loci before upstream screening and compared six genetically diverse isolates at 500 and 1,000 bp.

**Screen.** One 500 bp hit (1/32 resolved coding-conserved loci); no 1,000 bp hits (0/16). Class E contained 65 and 81 loci, respectively.

**Validation.** At Chr5_38, the original windows shared no alignment columns across the two isolate groups; the larger intervals shared 99.60–99.90% callable identity. The apparent variation came from comparing different parts of the locus.

**Takeaway.** A boundary-anchored screen can falsely suggest divergence when it compares non-equivalent sequence. The pilot did not establish the frequency of upstream variation; full mode was not run.
